You can book an appointment through three convenient methods:
  • Online & Mobile Banking: Log into your GeneriBank app and navigate to Support > Book a Branch Visit.
  • Telephone Banking: Call our UK customer service desk on 0800 123 4567.
  • In Branch: Speak with a host at any GeneriBank high street branch.

📄 What Identification & Documents to Bring

In accordance with UK FCA anti-money laundering guidelines, please bring one valid Proof of Identity and one Proof of Address:
  • Proof of ID: Valid UK Photocard Driving Licence, UK/EU Passport, or Biometric Residence Permit.
  • Proof of Address: Utility bill (gas, electric, water) dated within 3 months, Council Tax bill for current year, or HMRC tax notice.
Please prepare the following ahead of your meeting:
  • Your last 3 consecutive months of payslips (or 2 years of certified SA302 accounts if self-employed).
  • Your last 3 months of bank statements showing regular salary income and household expenditures.
  • Proof of deposit funds (e.g., savings account statements).
